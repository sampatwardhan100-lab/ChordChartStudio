Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Chord Chart Studio ("the app", "we") is a chord-chart editor created by Sameer Patwardhan. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

What we collect

  • Account details (only if you create an account): your name, email address and a password (stored by Google Firebase Authentication as a salted hash; we never see your password). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google.
  • Content you save to the cloud: song titles, chords, notes, comments, version history, setlists (their names and the songs in them), and the list of people a song or setlist is shared with (their names and emails).
  • Private notes: notes you mark "private" are stored under your account and can be read only by you.
  • Presence: while a shared song is open, your name, colour and the bar you are on are shown to other members of that song. This is deleted when you leave the song.
  • On your device: songs saved "on this device", automatic backups and settings are kept in your browser or app storage and are not sent to us.
  • PDF scores: PDFs and photos you add to PDF scores, and anything you write on them, are kept only in your browser or app storage on that device and are never uploaded to us. If you make a song entry from one, only its file name and page range are saved with that song, so someone you share it with must add the same file on their own device.

The app does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and we do not sell your information.

How it is used

Only to run the app: sign you in, store and sync your songs, show shared songs to the people you invite, and let you restore earlier versions.

Who can see your songs

Songs you save to the cloud are private to you until you share them. If you invite someone by email, or turn on a share link, those people (or anyone with the link who is signed in) can open the song with the access level you chose. You can change or remove access at any time from the Share window.

Service providers

Data is stored and processed by Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which may process data in locations outside your country. The app also loads open-source libraries from public CDNs (cdnjs, unpkg, Google Fonts/gstatic), which may receive your IP address as part of serving files. See Firebase privacy information.

Your choices and deletion

  • You can use the app without an account; nothing is then sent to the cloud.
  • Delete your account at any time in the app: account menu → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, the songs and setlists you own (with their comments and history), your private notes and your invitations, and removes you from songs and setlists shared with you.
  • You can export any song as PDF, image or file from the app.
  • When you import a PDF, MusicXML or picture of a chart, it is read inside your browser on your device and is not uploaded to our servers. For pictures, the text-recognition library and its English language data are downloaded from a public CDN (jsDelivr) the first time; the picture itself stays on your device.
  • Optional AI scan reading: only if you paste your own Anthropic API key and press "Read with AI", the page pictures of that chart are sent directly from your browser to Anthropic (api.anthropic.com) to be read. We never see them or your key. The key is stored only in your browser and you can remove it at any time.
  • For access, correction or other requests, contact us at the address below.

Practice audio

Audio files you add to a song are kept only in your browser on that device and are never uploaded to us. Links you add are saved with the song. A YouTube link loads the YouTube player, which is governed by YouTube's own terms and privacy policy. "Remember me" keeps you signed in on that device and remembers your email address there; we never store your password in the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and protected by access rules so that only members of a song can read it. No online service can be guaranteed perfectly secure.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: open an issue on the project page, github.com/sampatwardhan100-lab/ChordChartStudio/issues. Please do not post personal details in a public issue. You can delete your account and data yourself at any time from the account menu.
